04 The little things

Good to
know.

01

Get a driver for the long days

Budget $45–70 for 8–10 hours. It keeps temple days comfortable and traffic less mysterious.

02

Surf Kuta, dream Uluwatu

Kuta and Seminyak are kind to beginners. Save Uluwatu’s breaks for when your confidence is up.

03

Start early, leave room

Temples are cooler before 10am. Bali traffic is real, so one beautiful plan is better than three rushed ones.

04

Pack a sarong and some cash

Bring a cover-up for temples and keep $50–70/day flexible for tips, beach beds and little surprises.
